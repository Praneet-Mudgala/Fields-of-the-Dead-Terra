# Fields-of-the-Dead-Terra
Fields of the Dead is my submission for Terra Week 3 made on Godot Engine. It is a fast paced survival game where a child on Halloween night steps into a strange portal into the Fields of the Dead, where he is attacked by evil spectres and pyres and needs to defend himself with his wand or risk becoming a ghost himself... Controls are explained ingame!

## How to play
* Press this link made on Github Pages: https://praneet-mudgala.github.io/Fields-of-the-Dead-Terra/
* Press space to advance the story
* Use WASD or Arrow Keys to move
* Aim with your mouse
* Click or press space to shoot
* Have fun!
